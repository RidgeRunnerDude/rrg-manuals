Privacy Policy

Ridge Runner Gear respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use our website, contact us, request information, or purchase products, and how that information may be used.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information You Provide

Ridge Runner Gear may receive personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact us through the website.
  • Ask a question about a product.
  • Request warranty service or a return.
  • Place or arrange an order.

  • Communicate with us by email or another supported method.

Depending on the situation, this information may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing or shipping address, order information, and any information you choose to include in your message.

This may include information such as:

  • General technical information used for security and operation.

Information may be used to:

  • Provide product support.

Payment information submitted directly to a third-party payment provider is subject to that provider's own privacy and security practices.
