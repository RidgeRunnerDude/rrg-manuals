Warranty & Return Policy

At Ridge Runner Gear, we design and manufacture gear intended to be used, carried, tested, and taken outdoors. We stand behind our products and want you to be satisfied with your purchase.

90-Day Limited Warranty

Warranty Coverage

Ridge Runner Gear warrants its products to be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal use for a period of 90 days from the original date of purchase.

What Is Not Covered

This warranty applies only to the original purchaser and does not cover damage resulting from misuse, abuse, accidents, unauthorized modifications, improper maintenance, improper installation, exposure to conditions beyond the product's intended use, or normal wear and tear.

How We Handle Warranty Claims

If a covered defect occurs during the warranty period, Ridge Runner Gear will, at its discretion, repair the product, replace the defective component, replace the product, or provide another appropriate remedy.

Proof of purchase may be required.

Submitting a Warranty Claim

To submit a warranty claim, contact Ridge Runner Gear through our website. Please include your name, order information, a description of the issue, and photos when appropriate.

30-Day Return Policy

Returns & Exchanges

If you are not satisfied with your Ridge Runner Gear purchase, you may request a return or exchange within 30 days of the original purchase date.

Light Testing Is Okay

We understand that outdoor gear sometimes needs to be handled or lightly tested before you know whether it is right for you.

Products may be lightly used for reasonable evaluation, but they must be returned in good condition with all originally included components.

Return Condition

Normal signs of reasonable testing are acceptable. Products that are damaged, abused, excessively worn, modified, altered, or returned with missing components may be subject to a reduced refund or may not qualify for a refund.

Before Sending a Return

Please contact Ridge Runner Gear before sending any product back. We will provide return authorization and return instructions.

Return Shipping

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the product arrived damaged, defective, incorrect, or the return is the result of an error by Ridge Runner Gear.

Refunds

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to a Ridge Runner Gear error or a covered warranty issue.

Refunds will be issued after the returned product has been received and inspected.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders

Let Us Know

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or contains the wrong item, please contact us as soon as possible.

Photos May Help

We may ask for photographs of the product and packaging so we can determine the best way to resolve the issue.

We'll Make It Right

When Ridge Runner Gear is responsible for the problem, we will work with you to make it right.

A Word About 3D-Printed Products

Proudly 3D Printed

Many Ridge Runner Gear products are proudly manufactured using 3D printing.

Normal Printing Characteristics

Because of the nature of the 3D-printing process, products may have visible layer lines, small surface variations, minor gaps, seam marks, or slight differences in weight, texture, or appearance.

Color shades may also vary slightly between material batches.

What Counts as a Defect?

These characteristics are normal and are not considered manufacturing defects unless they materially affect the intended function or performance of the product.

Function Over Form

Ridge Runner Gear is a function-over-form company, but that does not mean appearance is ignored.

Every product is inspected with the goal of providing durable, functional gear that we would be comfortable taking into the field ourselves.
