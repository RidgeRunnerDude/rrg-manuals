Our Story

Ridge Runner Gear began in the mountains of Blue Ridge, Georgia, at the southern edge of the Appalachian range.

Around there, locals joke that mountain people are “ridge runners” with one leg longer than the other from walking hillsides their whole lives. Growing up in those mountains builds a deep appreciation for the outdoors—and for the gear you rely on when you’re far from the trailhead.

After retiring from a career as a mechanical design engineer, I began spending more time doing something I’ve always loved: luxury base-camp backpacking.

I enjoy the wilderness, but I also appreciate a few creature comforts while roughing it. On trips with friends, we kept finding ourselves saying the same thing:

“Wouldn’t it be nice if…”

Wouldn’t it be nice if one piece of gear could do two jobs?

Wouldn’t it be nice if we could carry less but still have everything we need?

Wouldn’t it be nice if this simply worked better?

Those questions became Ridge Runner Gear.

Design rugged outdoor tools that do more, weigh less, and make the experience better.

From CAD to Campfire

Ridge Runner Gear is a small workshop brand built around real use, real engineering, and real field experience.

Every product starts with a problem worth solving. From there it becomes a detailed CAD model, moves through prototyping and field testing, and is refined until the design does what it was intended to do.

Only then is it ready to become Ridge Runner Gear.

Engineering & Design

Every Ridge Runner product begins as a detailed engineering model in Creo.

Design comes first. Every feature should have a reason to exist, and every part should have a purpose before it ever reaches the trail.

Modern Manufacturing

Precision parts are produced using advanced 3D-printing technology that allows us to rapidly test ideas, improve designs, and manufacture dependable products in small batches.

That gives us something large-scale manufacturing often doesn’t: the freedom to keep improving.

Small-Batch Assembly

Components are assembled, inspected, and finished by hand.

Small-batch production allows us to pay attention to the details and make sure each piece of gear is ready for practical use before it leaves our shop.

The Ridge Runner Workbench

This is where gear gets assembled, tested, adjusted, broken, improved, and tested again.

And every now and then, a future Ridge Runner product sneaks onto the workbench before it’s officially ready for the world.

What Makes RRG Different

We believe outdoor gear should earn its place in your pack.

If a product doesn’t make a trip lighter, simpler, more capable, or more enjoyable, it probably doesn’t belong in the Ridge Runner Gear lineup.

Our designs focus on practical function, multi-use capability, repairability, and long-term value. We want gear that works hard, lasts a long time, and can be repaired or refurbished whenever practical instead of simply being thrown away.

Whenever possible, we use American-sourced materials and components. Materials are selected for what they contribute to the design and how they perform outdoors, including:

  • ASA for UV and weather resistance
  • TPU for flexibility and impact resistance
  • Stainless steel for corrosion-resistant strength

We don’t add features just so we can add them to a product description.

If something is there, it should have a reason to be there.

What We Believe

At Ridge Runner Gear, we believe you shouldn’t have to choose between comfort and capability.

We build gear for people who appreciate practical tools, thoughtful design, and trail-ready durability without carrying more than they need.

Luxury in the outdoors isn’t about excess.

It’s about having the right gear—the kind that makes camp life easier, more efficient, and more enjoyable.

Some folks call it roughing it.

We just think it ought to work better.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to design the gear we wish existed and build the company we wish we could buy from.

We believe in:

Standardizing the repeatable.
Innovating the meaningful.
And enjoying the impossible becoming routine.

Ridge Runner Gear exists to create thoughtful, practical outdoor products that solve real problems and genuinely improve people’s outdoor experiences.

Our goal isn’t simply to sell more gear. It’s to build a sustainable business that earns enough to keep doing what we love most—creating new products, refining good ideas, solving problems, and finding better ways to help people enjoy the outdoors.

Build Your System, One Adventure at a Time

Ridge Runner Gear products are designed to work together.

Start with the core gear you need today, then expand your system as your adventures grow. Add capability when you need it without having to buy everything at once.

The result is gear that grows with you rather than gear you outgrow.

We believe the outdoors is one of God’s greatest gifts, and we’re passionate about helping others enjoy it responsibly and respectfully.

That’s why we practice Leave No Trace principles and strive to be good stewards of the wild places we love.

We also believe that a good name is worth more than gold.

Designed and Built in Georgia. Trail Ready.

Ridge Runner Gear products are designed, manufactured, assembled, and tested in Georgia with rugged durability and refined utility for people who actually use their gear.

Whenever practical, we also choose American-sourced materials and components while selecting each material for the job it needs to do.

We’re not interested in making more stuff just to make more stuff.

A Note From Tony

Ridge Runner Gear didn’t start with a business plan.

It started around campfires, on trails, and during those moments when somebody looked at a piece of gear and said:

“Wouldn’t it be nice if…”

For a design engineer, that’s a dangerous question—because once I see a problem, I have a hard time leaving it alone.

Every Ridge Runner product begins with that same idea: there has to be a better way.

Sometimes the answer is making something lighter. Sometimes it’s making one tool do the job of two. Sometimes it’s creating an entirely new solution because the thing we wanted simply didn’t exist.

That’s the part of Ridge Runner Gear I enjoy most.

Taking something that once seemed difficult—or even impossible—and working on it until one day it becomes routine.

Thank you for supporting Ridge Runner Gear and allowing us to keep creating.

— Tony
Founder & Designer, Ridge Runner Gear
